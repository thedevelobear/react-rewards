# 0069. The pop blooms in three layers, placed in closed form and timed by its lifetime

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-31

- **Settings.** The only settings are `count`, `dots`, `size`, `radius`, `swirl`, `lifetime`, `bounce` and `colors`. A pop has no `velocity`, `drag`, `angle`, `spread` or `gravity`: it always goes all the way round, `radius` sets how far it reaches, and `lifetime` sets its pace.
- **Layers.** `count` is stars. On its first step each star adds its streak, and the first star adds the flash and a ring of `dots` dots. Each is set up as it would be at that moment, and keeps the moment in a slot, so it stays in step with the stars at any frame rate.
- **Stars.** They alternate long and short around the circle, each within 0.12 of the middle of its own share, and take the colours in turn. They start 0.2 × `radius` out. Long stars land at `radius` and short ones at 0.62 × `radius`, each ±6%. One draw sets both the reach and the size (±10%), so bigger stars fly further. Short stars are 0.7 of the size and launch at 0.06 of `lifetime`.
- **The path.** Stars and streaks are placed on their path, with their velocity at 0, not pushed by it. A star has covered `q = 1 - exp(-k·τ)` of its way τ seconds after its launch, with `k = 6 / lifetime`. It is then `from + (to - from)·q` out, turned `swirl·q` counter-clockwise, so it comes to rest `swirl` round from where it set off. The swirl turns the same way in every pop: counter-clockwise for a positive `swirl`.
- **The streak.** A streak shows where its star was `ln 3 / k` seconds ago. It reaches back to the start until the star is two thirds of the way, then it is twice as long as the way left, so it shrinks into the star as the star stops. Its length is measured along the path, it points along the tangent halfway along, and it fades once it is shorter than its star.
- **The star's look.** While it leads its streak, a star is a tip at 0.35 of its size. From 0.25 to 0.5 of `lifetime` after its launch it blooms, overshooting to 1.16 on an ease out that peaks at 1.25. That ease lives in the effect's folder and is not a public math helper ([ADR 0053](0053-public-math-helpers.md)). After the bloom it twinkles 3 to 4.5 times per `lifetime`, dipping to 0.6 of its size and 0.75 of its opacity. On top of its path's turn, it turns about a further 1.5 × `swirl` by the end. It ends at 0.92 to 1 times `lifetime`, shrinking to nothing over the last 0.25.
- **The ring.** Its dots are evenly spaced, turned half their spacing from the first star's line, so they fly between the long streaks: with 10 stars and 5 dots, along the short streaks' lines. When `dots` is a multiple of the number of long streaks, each gap between them holds the same dots, centred in it. Otherwise they stay evenly spaced and symmetric about the first star's line, and one sets off along a long streak's line only when `count` is even and `count / 2` has more factors of 2 than `dots`, as with 8 stars and 2 dots. With an odd half `count`, as at 10, that never happens. It races from 0.3 to 1.15 × `radius` with `k = 8 / lifetime`. Its dots thin towards 0.45 of their size (0.47 when it is spent), fade as `1 - x²`, and are gone at 0.45 of `lifetime`.
- **The flash.** It is 1.2 × `radius` across at the centre, grows from 0.6 of that to its full size, and fades linearly from 0.85 over 0.12 of `lifetime`. It draws over the stars, since particles are drawn in the order they were added, and the first star adds it on its first step. It is a light, so it reads right on top.
- **The element.** With `bounce`, the pop animates the element it fires from ([ADR 0073](0073-effects-animate-their-element.md)), like the heart of the CSS-Tricks recreation of Twitter's. The element shrinks to nothing by 0.03 of `lifetime` (ease-in), and stays hidden while the flash and the ring burst. From 0.175 it springs back on `cubic-bezier(0.3, 1.9, 0.6, 1)`, overshooting to 1.21 at 0.377, as the long stars' bloom peaks at 0.375. It settles at full size by 0.6, with no jolt, since the curve ends flat. The animation lasts 0.6 of `lifetime`. While the element is shrunk to nothing, 45 to 262 ms in at the defaults, a tap lands on whatever is under it. If the button must stay tappable, the ref should point at the icon inside it, as Twitter's heart does.
- **Timing.** Every time is a share of `lifetime`, so a longer pop plays the same pop slower. At 1500 ms the flash lasts 0.18 s (5 frames at 30 Hz) and the ring 0.675 s. Stars cover 63% of their way by 0.25 s and 95% by 0.75 s. A streak is longest at 0.275 s, 2/3 of the way: 42.7 px for a long star. Long stars' bloom peaks at 0.56 s, and stars twinkle every 0.33 to 0.5 s. The element is gone by 0.045 s, hidden until 0.26 s, at 1.21 at 0.57 s, and settled by 0.9 s.
- **Sprites.** Each colour has two sprites: a four-point star with concave sides and a round halo, and a needle streak whose head is at the middle of its box, so stretching it never reaches past its star. The first colour also has a haloed dot for the ring, and the flash, which is paler in the middle. A dot is 0.2 × `size` across and a streak 0.16 × `size` at its widest, so the ring stays fine and the streaks thin beside the stars. Halos are gradients of the colour as the canvas reads it back, as the firework's glows are ([ADR 0067](0067-fireworks-climb-in-closed-form.md)). A colour that can't be read back is drawn without a halo.
- **Defaults.** `count` 10, `dots` 5, `size` 18, `radius` 80, `swirl` 20, `lifetime` 1500, `bounce` on, and the colours rose `#FF4D79`, amber `#F59E0B`, violet `#8B5CF6` and sky `#1FB2F5`. Each colour has at least 2:1 contrast on white and 4.5:1 on `#0a0a0b`. At `radius` 80, around a 52 px round button, the short stars land 8 to 14 px clear of the button's edge even at its 1.21 overshoot, where at 60 they would sit on it. A pop peaks at 26 particles, two per star with 5 dots and the flash, and loads 10 sprites.

**Why:** Placed in closed form, a pop matches itself within 0.00003 px at any frame rate, and its stars land exactly at `radius`. The core's trapezoid move would overshoot a path this strongly damped by 0.5% at 30 Hz at the defaults, and by more at larger radii. Timing by `lifetime` keeps the layers' rhythm at any length, and keeps the element's spring in step with the stars. A streak tied to where its star was shrinks into the star by itself, with no crossfade.

**Consequences:** A star is two particles, and each pop adds `dots + 1` more, so the Stress test and `pnpm bench` ask for one star per 2 particles. The colour readback and the hash that draws a star's reach, size and twinkle from its seed are copies of the firework's, because effects don't import each other. Below a `lifetime` of about 560 ms, the flash lasts under 2 frames at 30 Hz.

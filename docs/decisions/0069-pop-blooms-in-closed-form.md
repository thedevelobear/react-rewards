# 0069. The pop blooms in three layers, placed in closed form and timed by its lifetime

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-31

- **Layers.** `count` is stars. On its first step each star adds its streak, and the first star adds the flash and a ring of `dots` dots. Each is set up as it would be at that moment, and keeps the moment in a slot, so it stays in step with the stars at any frame rate.
- **Stars.** They alternate long and short around the circle, each within 0.12 of the middle of its own share, and take the colours in turn. They start 0.2 × `radius` out. Long stars land at `radius` and short ones at 0.62 × `radius`, each ±6%. One draw sets both the reach and the size (±10%), so bigger stars fly further. Short stars are 0.7 of the size and launch at 0.06 of `lifetime`.
- **The path.** Stars and streaks are placed on their path, with their velocity at 0, not pushed by it. A star has covered `q = 1 - exp(-k·τ)` of its way τ seconds after its launch, with `k = 6 / lifetime`. It is then `from + (to - from)·q` out, turned `swirl·q` counter-clockwise, so it comes to rest `swirl` round from where it set off.
- **The streak.** A streak shows where its star was `ln 3 / k` seconds ago. It reaches back to the start until the star is two thirds of the way, then it is twice as long as the way left, so it shrinks into the star as the star stops. Its length is measured along the path, it points along the tangent halfway along, and it fades once it is shorter than its star.
- **The star's look.** While it leads its streak, a star is a tip at 0.35 of its size. From 0.25 to 0.5 of `lifetime` after its launch it blooms, overshooting to 1.16 on an ease out that peaks at 1.25. That ease lives in the effect's folder and is not a public math helper ([ADR 0053](0053-public-math-helpers.md)). After the bloom it twinkles 3 to 4.5 times per `lifetime`, dipping to 0.6 of its size and 0.75 of its opacity. On top of its path's turn, it turns about a further 1.5 × `swirl` by the end. It ends at 0.92 to 1 times `lifetime`, shrinking to nothing over the last 0.25.
- **The ring.** It races from 0.3 to 1.15 × `radius` with `k = 8 / lifetime`. Its dots thin towards 0.45 of their size (0.47 when it is spent), fade as `1 - x²`, and are gone at 0.45 of `lifetime`.
- **The flash.** It is 1.2 × `radius` across at the centre, grows from 0.6 of that to its full size, and fades linearly from 0.85 over 0.12 of `lifetime`.
- **Timing.** Every time is a share of `lifetime`, so a longer pop plays the same pop slower. At 800 ms the flash lasts 96 ms (3 frames at 30 Hz) and the ring 0.36 s. Stars cover 63% of their way by 0.13 s and 95% by 0.4 s. A streak is longest at 0.15 s, 2/3 of the way: 32 px for a long star. Stars twinkle every 0.178 to 0.267 s.
- **Sprites.** Each colour has two sprites: a four-point star with concave sides and a round halo, and a needle streak whose head is at the middle of its box, so stretching it never reaches past its star. The first colour also has a haloed dot for the ring, and the flash, which is paler in the middle. Halos are gradients of the colour as the canvas reads it back, as the firework's glows are ([ADR 0067](0067-fireworks-climb-in-closed-form.md)). A colour that can't be read back is drawn without a halo.
- **Defaults.** `count` 10, `dots` 24, `size` 10, `radius` 60, `swirl` 20, `lifetime` 800, and the colours rose `#FF4D79`, amber `#F59E0B`, violet `#8B5CF6` and sky `#1FB2F5`. Each colour has at least 2:1 contrast on white and 4.5:1 on `#0a0a0b`. A pop peaks at 45 particles and loads 10 sprites.

**Why:** Placed in closed form, a pop matches itself within 0.00003 px at any frame rate, and its stars land exactly at `radius`. The core's trapezoid move would overshoot a path this strongly damped by 0.5% at 30 Hz at the defaults, and by more at larger radii. Timing by `lifetime` keeps the layers' rhythm at any length. A streak tied to where its star was shrinks into the star by itself, with no crossfade.

**Consequences:** A star is two particles, and each pop adds `dots + 1` more, so the Stress test and `pnpm bench` ask for one star per 2 particles. The colour readback and the hash that draws a star's reach, size and twinkle from its seed are copies of the firework's, because effects don't import each other. Below a `lifetime` of about 560 ms, the flash lasts under 2 frames at 30 Hz.
